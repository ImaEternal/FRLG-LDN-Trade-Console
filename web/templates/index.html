On your Switch
  1. Open the Direct Corner and choose to trade
  2. Be the Leader, and stay on the waiting screen
  3. Approve the join request from EMU
  4. Walk to the left chair — movement may be laggy
  5. Pick any Pokémon from your own party to give, and accept
  6. Back at the trade menu, cancel, then walk out

Keep the console on the waiting screen the whole time — it only advertises while you are stood there.
